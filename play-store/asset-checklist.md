# Store Asset Checklist

Use only screenshots and graphics captured from the real app experience. Do not show capabilities that are not present in the production build.

## Required assets

- App icon: 512 x 512 PNG, within Play Console size limits.
- Feature graphic: 1024 x 500.
- Phone screenshots: at least two.
- Tablet screenshots: add if tablets are supported in Play Console.
- Short video: optional, only if it shows the real app.

## Screenshot guidance

- Show the model library.
- Show a local chat with neutral, non-sensitive sample text.
- Show the settings/privacy area.
- Do not show copyrighted model logos unless license allows it.
- Do not show fake medical, legal, financial, or emergency advice claims.
- Do not imply all AI output is private if model downloads or reports contact external services.

## Google Play Metadata Policy Compliance (Strict)

Google Play automated visual filters run OCR over all uploaded screenshots and graphics. To avoid metadata rejections:

- **Prohibited Ranking Claims**: Never use words like *"Best"*, *"#1"*, *"Top"*, *"Leading"*, *"Fastest"*, or *"App of the Year"* anywhere in screenshot banners or sample chat text.
- **Prohibited Pricing Claims**: Never use promotional words like *"Free"*, *"Deal"*, *"Save"*, *"Sale"*, or *"Discount"*.
- **No Store Badges or Ratings**: Never include Google Play logos, award badges, star ratings, or user review quotes in screenshot artwork.
- **Authentic App Experience**: Screenshots must directly reflect actual screens from the running build. Avoid 3D marketing renders or exaggerated mockups that obscure the UI.
- **Neutral Sample Chat Text**: Ensure mock questions and answers shown in chat bubbles do not contain blacklisted trigger words.

## Final review

- Text must be readable on small phone screenshots.
- No debug UI, local file paths, test endpoints, or placeholder policy URLs.
- Screenshots must match the submitted build.
- Screen every image against the metadata policy checklist above before uploading to Play Console.
