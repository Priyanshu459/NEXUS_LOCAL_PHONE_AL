## Release requirements

- Keep license text or links available in the app or linked support material.
- Do not imply the app owns or created third-party models.
- Do not hide gated, restricted, or custom license obligations behind a generic "open source" claim.
- Re-check model repositories before each release; model hosting, filenames, and licenses can change.
